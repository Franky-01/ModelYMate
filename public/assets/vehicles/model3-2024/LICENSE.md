## ModelYMate runtime adaptation, 2026-11-12

The delivery GLB uses lossless meshopt compression; decoded vertex and index data match the acquired source. ModelYMate's runtime adapter normalizes scale, orientation and ground position; separates original geometry into opening panels and four wheel groups; defines hinge and steering pivots; splits the charging-flap surface where required; adds a small fixed socket recess; and adjusts material transparency, paint and lighting response. Interior geometry, UVs and source textures are retained. Exterior and inner body paint color maps are disabled to support user-selected paint. The 2024 wheel finish uses solid satin/alloy materials, and its intersecting outer black inserts are omitted at runtime while keeping the original complete alloy wheel, tire, brake and center cover. The acquired/delivered GLB retains those inserts. These modifications are not endorsed by the original author or Tesla.
